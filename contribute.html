If you want to contribute, just drag and drop or upload your Amity University PYQs here

Steps before uploading PYQs

Help us grow a free question paper archive for Amity University! If you have previous year papers not already on AmiPYQ, just upload here and get credit on our site.

How to Contribute

  1. Scan Your Paper
    Use mobile apps (Adobe Scan, Clear Scanner, MS Lens) or PC (NAPS2).
    Black & white/greyscale only. Scan one page at a time. Export as PDF.
  2. Name the File
    Format: subjectname_subjectcode_year_month.pdf
    Example: appliedmathematicsIII_math211_2023_dec.pdf
    Use lowercase and underscores.
  3. Upload PDF
    Drag & Drop
    Or Upload
    Note:
    - Must be in PDF format
    - Must named in the format as mentioned
    - Must be less than 5mb
